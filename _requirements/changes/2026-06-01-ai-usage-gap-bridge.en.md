# Requirement Change: AI Usage Gap And Bridge

## Change

- Add `REQ-WS-042`

## Background

The user asked to research the difference between strong and weak AI users and reflect the bridge into the platform.

## Requirement

AI-use gaps should be diagnosed as context, task-fit, iteration, verification, tooling, or asset-promotion gaps rather than personal flaws. The platform should apply the smallest useful bridge intervention and promote reusable patterns into durable assets.
