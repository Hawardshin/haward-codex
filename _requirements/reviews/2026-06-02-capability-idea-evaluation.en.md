# Requirement Review: Capability Idea Evaluation

## Review Result

- Status: approved
- Requirement: `REQ-WS-070`

## Judgment

If idea generation is automated without evaluation, the first generated solution can become overbuilt. Separating idea generation from evaluation, and recording selection and rejection reasons, fits the existing bounded black-box principle.

## Approval Conditions

- Generate several ideas for non-trivial problems.
- Keep evaluation criteria and scores.
- Keep rejection or queue reasons for unselected ideas.
