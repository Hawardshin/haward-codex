# Principle Guardian Agent Research Note

## Conclusion

`principle-guardian-agent` should not merely explain principles. It should act as a governance agent that stops shortcuts and requires rework when principles erode.

## Evidence Summary

- NIST AI RMF provides a governance, risk mapping, measurement, and management frame for trustworthy AI.
- ISO/IEC 42001 treats AI management as policies, objectives, processes, and continual improvement.
- OECD AI Principles emphasize accountability, transparency, safety, and robustness.
- High Reliability Organization principles emphasize preoccupation with failure, reluctance to simplify, and sensitivity to operations.

## Agent Design Principles

- Principles are close-out gates, not slogans.
- Speed, money, optimism, and convenience cannot bypass principles.
- Principle conflicts must record source and decision reason.
- Exceptions must be documented only when permitted, and reversible paths are preferred.
- Meaningful work must preserve the required evidence, source provenance, validation, and evaluation.
