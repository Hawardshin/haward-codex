# Research Note: Source Discovery Expansion

## Summary

This expansion strengthens the seed set so research does not depend on a single generic web search. It separates global enterprise/product engineering blogs, Korean big-tech and product engineering blogs, Indian product-company and people/creator sources, and paper-discovery origins.

## Key Decisions

- Globally reusable high-signal engineering sources were also added to `enterprise-source-registry.json`.
- Korean, India, people, and paper source origins remain governed by `source-discovery-registry.json`.
- Individual blogs, YouTube, Reddit/LinkedIn/upvote/star signals are adoption, discovery, or context signals only.
- Paper research should combine multiple channels across graph indexes, publisher/venue indexes, preprints or publisher pages, and code/adoption signals.

## Representative Additions

- Global: Shopify, Slack, Databricks, Discord, Vercel, Datadog, Sentry, Canva, Figma, DoorDash
- Korea: LY Corporation, Daangn, Devsisters, RIDI plus existing NAVER D2/Kakao/Toss/Woowa/Kurly/LINE/Coupang/KakaoBank sources
- India: Razorpay Engineering, Zerodha, Flipkart, PhonePe, CRED, Meesho, Zomato, Arpit Bhayani, Kailash Nadh, Gaurav Sen
- Papers: DBLP, ACM, IEEE, USENIX, NeurIPS, PMLR, ICLR OpenReview, ACL Anthology, Crossref, ResearchRabbit, Litmaps, Hugging Face Papers

## Usage Caveats

- A source list is a search starting point, not proof.
- Reopen the exact article or paper page and record access date plus extraction notes before using a claim.
- Record regional market/user/operating fit before using regional practice as planning evidence.
- For famous papers, inspect citations, venue, code, benchmark, reproducibility, and contrary or follow-up work together.
