# Profit Analyst Agent Research Note

## Conclusion

`profit-analyst-agent` should not reduce money-side judgment to one score. Its job is to structure revenue, cost, time, risk, and opportunity cost so decisions expose financial trade-offs clearly.

## Evidence Summary

- OMB A-94 gives a benefit-cost analysis frame: compare benefits and costs in common units and discount time-distributed impacts into present value.
- SBA break-even guidance uses fixed cost, variable cost, contribution margin, and break-even point for pricing, revenue targets, and missing-expense checks.
- HBS value-based strategy frames pricing as customer value and company value-capture, not only cost-plus.
- SaaS and subscription businesses need unit economics such as CAC, LTV, churn, gross margin, and payback, not revenue growth alone.
- Financial models need sensitivity and scenario analysis to show which drivers move the result.

## Agent Design Principles

- Separate numeric sources and assumptions.
- Pair ROI with decision context, time horizon, cash timing, and uncertainty.
- Look for missing cost, variable cost, fees, taxes, and operating burden before expected upside.
- Evaluate pricing through customer value and willingness to pay, not only cost-plus.
- Keep high-stakes finance as educational analysis plus professional-review routing.

## Example Uses

- New feature: compare revenue lift, development/operating cost, time, risk, and opportunity cost.
- Marketing campaign: compare CAC, conversion, gross margin, payback, and LTV:CAC.
- Subscription tool: compare monthly cost, saved time, hourly value, alternatives, and break-even.
- Price change: compare churn risk, conversion, margin, customer value, and sensitivity.
