# Plan: Human-Process Automation Purpose

## Work Mode

- `governance`

## Evidence

- Google SRE toil guidance provides a standard for reducing repetitive, automatable manual work so more time can go to long-term engineering work.
- The Google SRE workbook frames toil reduction as identifying, quantifying, and improving repetition with ROI and risk in mind.
- IBM LiveAction research shows that people may not recognize their own repetition, while real usage data can reveal automatable repeated behavior.
- IBM RPA and Microsoft process/task mining materials describe turning repeated human actions and process data into automation opportunities.

## Steps

1. Check web evidence and existing repository documents.
2. Update the requirement baseline, change record, and review record.
3. Update philosophy, identity, README, and governance documents.
4. Reflect the purpose into persistent instructions, AGENTS, and memory bootstrap.
5. Create spec, research note, plan, request summary, trace, work summary, and timing record.
6. Validate configs, memory, docs, naming, structure, grounding, evaluator, and work timer.
7. Commit and push the meaningful change set.

## Bottleneck Candidates

- The purpose spans several document layers, so keeping wording consistent without duplicating policy too broadly may take the most time.
- The new rule must preserve validation and rollback boundaries so it does not become “automate everything” guidance.
