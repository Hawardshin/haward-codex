## Plan Impact

- Strong principle adherence is defined as governance, risk management, accountability, documented process, and continuous improvement rather than motivational wording.
- The new role is a `principle-guardian-agent` for principle conflicts, shortcuts, and close-out checks, not a separate runtime above every agent.
- When speed, money, optimism, or convenience conflicts with principles, the agent preserves the principle and requires a compliant alternative.

## Uncertainty

- The ISO/IEC 42001 full text is a paid standard, so only the public official page was summarized.
- Real principle conflicts are context-specific, so the policy keeps human decision inbox routing and reversible paths.
