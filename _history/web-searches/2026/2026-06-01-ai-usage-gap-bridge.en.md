## Weak Sources Ignored

- Productivity posts with no methodology or source trail.
- Social posts that make unsupported claims about pay or time savings.
- Vendor marketing pages selling a specific AI tool.
- Reddit/community posts were treated as discovery signals only, not factual proof.

## Insights Applied

- Strong AI use is not just prompt wording; it is task-fit judgment, context quality, iteration, verification, durable assets, and calibrated reliance.
- AI-use gaps should be framed as workflow gaps rather than personal flaws.
- The platform should promote effective AI-use patterns into prompts, workflows, templates, tools, skills, and configs.
- AI outputs should be neither blindly trusted nor rejected; risk-appropriate verification paths should be attached.

## Public Decision Summary

This change turns strong AI-use behavior into a platform profile and bridge workflow instead of leaving it as generic advice.
