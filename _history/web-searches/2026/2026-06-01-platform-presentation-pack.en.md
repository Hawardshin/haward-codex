## Weak Sources Ignored

- Generic SEO-style “best PPT template” pages were treated as visual inspiration only.
- Reddit/community advice was treated only as discovery signal and not factual evidence for the deck.

## Plan Impact

- The overall platform deck follows problem, shift, operating loop, project portfolio, and next-action structure.
- Project decks use a consistent shape while separately covering purpose, scope, key files, artifacts, validation, and next actions.
- Detailed operating content goes into speaker notes to reduce omissions without overloading slides.
- HTML outputs are generated with the existing `presentation_agent.html_deck` renderer so notes, progress, and keyboard navigation are included.

## Uncertainty

- The final audience and presentation duration are not fixed. This pack is a comprehensive preparation version and can later be shortened for a specific venue.
