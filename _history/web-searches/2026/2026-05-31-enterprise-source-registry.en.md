## Weak Or Excluded Sources

- SEO-style "best engineering blogs" lists were used only as discovery signals.
- Wikipedia, Reddit, and general news were treated only as secondary discovery signals.
- Aggregators were used only after confirming official URLs.

## Insights Applied

- Official company, research lab, and architecture center sources should be separated from the general source taxonomy as reusable seed lists.
- The list itself is not proof; exact source pages must be re-opened before citing specific claims.
- Large-company examples have scale, organizational, and vendor bias, so each registry entry needs `caveats`.

## Remaining Uncertainty

- Some sites may change URL structure or publication cadence.
- Future domain-specific high-quality sources should be added when discovered.
