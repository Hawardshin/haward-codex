## Plan Impact

- Positive vision should not be only emotional encouragement; it should produce an execution brief containing `agency`, `pathways`, `goal`, `if-then plan`, and `reality check`.
- "Somehow make it happen" can be misread as unsupported guarantees or bypassing quality and safety, so the policy requires factual grounding, risk disclosure, fallback paths, and human decision inbox routing.
- `timekeeper-agent` handles time pressure; `positive-vision-agent` preserves morale and possibility while turning pressure into paths.

## Uncertainty

- The effect of positive vision depends on context, team culture, user state, and goal difficulty.
- This work adds a reusable agent spec and operating documentation, not a standalone runtime.
