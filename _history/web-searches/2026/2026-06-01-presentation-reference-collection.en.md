## Weak Or Risky Sources Ignored

- Reddit requests to share/download premium Slidesgo templates were not used because they create legal and ethical risk.
- Likes/views from design galleries were treated only as visual discovery signals, not proof or reuse permission.
- Account-gated or paid templates were excluded from raw local storage.

## Plan Impact

- Prioritize `native_html` frameworks such as reveal.js, Slidev, and Marp for HTML presentation output.
- Store PPT/PPTX template provenance and license status first; only download or convert after per-item license review.
- Manage presentation assets as `asset_reference` first and store raw assets only after terms review.
- Start PPTX conversion with a Python text-structure extractor; evaluate high-fidelity converters later through separate research and installation audit.

## Uncertainty

- Download terms may vary by individual template.
- Open-source converter maintenance and output quality require installation-time verification.
- Photo/icon/font licenses may vary by source-wide terms and individual asset terms.
