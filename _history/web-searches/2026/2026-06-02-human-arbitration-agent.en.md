## Plan Impact

- Human arbitration must be separated from missing evidence. If facts are unresolved, route back to research, grounding, or knowledge skepticism first.
- When facts are sufficiently grounded but values, risk appetite, strategy, or accountability remain, route to human arbitration.
- The human handoff should be a small decision packet with options, evidence, trade-offs, recommended default, blocked/unblocked work, and resume action.

## Remaining Uncertainty

- Actual human choices depend on user authority, risk appetite, and project context.
- This change creates settings, docs, requirements, and evaluation structure first, not a runtime arbitration engine.
