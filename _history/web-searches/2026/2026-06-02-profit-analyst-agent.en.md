## Plan Impact

- Money-side judgment should not end with one ROI number; it should selectively include cost-benefit, NPV, payback, break-even, unit economics, pricing value, opportunity cost, and scenario/sensitivity analysis.
- Numeric values must separate source, formula, date, currency, tax/fee inclusion, and assumptions.
- Personal investment, tax, legal, accounting, and regulated finance are high-risk domains requiring professional review or a human checkpoint.
- Money upside must not override safety, law, privacy, user trust, or quality.

## Uncertainty

- Benchmarks, prices, tax rates, exchange rates, platform fees, and market data change over time and require fresh search before real decisions.
- This work adds a reusable agent spec and operating docs, not a standalone financial-modeling runtime.
