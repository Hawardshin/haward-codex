## Weak Or Excluded Sources

- Full map-review scraping: excluded from default tool behavior because official API/terms boundaries are unclear.
- SEO-style “best tech blogs” lists: useful for discovery only; registry entries prefer official or primary pages.
- Social/personal posts: discovery signals only, not standalone factual evidence.

## Insights Applied

- Korean user review/local-market decisions may need Naver Map, Naver Blog/Search, and Kakao Map before Google.
- Map/blog reviews are user-experience signals; factual claims should be cross-checked through official pages or map place information.
- Paper evidence should combine Semantic Scholar/OpenAlex/arXiv/Papers with Code to evaluate influence, freshness, and code availability.
- Source discovery should stay in a separate registry rather than overloading the general source taxonomy.

## Remaining Uncertainty

- Naver/Kakao review access depends on API policy and service terms, which may change.
- Some company blog URLs may move; exact pages must be rechecked before use.
