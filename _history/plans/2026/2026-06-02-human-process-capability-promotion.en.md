The user asked that capability promotion and idea evaluation work “as if a person directly did it.”

## Selected Approach

“Human-like” is interpreted as reproducing the work sequence and artifacts of a competent worker, not role-playing a human tone. Capability candidates should first model direct human work, then turn repeated steps into generated and evaluated ideas.
