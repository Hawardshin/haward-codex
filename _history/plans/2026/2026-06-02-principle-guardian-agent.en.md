Turn "everyone strongly adheres to principles" into a persistent operating rule and reusable governance agent.

## Steps

1. Run web research on AI governance, responsible AI principles, management systems, and high reliability principles.
2. Inspect existing principles, requirements, and agent structure.
3. Add durable requirement `REQ-WS-067`.
4. Add the `principle-guardian-agent` config and Korean/English docs.
5. Add principle-adherence rules to persistent instructions and README.
6. Add spec, plan, tasks, validation, and traceability artifacts.
7. Run verification and evaluation, then commit and push.
