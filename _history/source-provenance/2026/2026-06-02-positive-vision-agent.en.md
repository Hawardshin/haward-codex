## Applied Judgment

- Positive framing is treated as a goal-pursuit structure, not only an emotional intervention.
- Possibility framing should include both agency and pathways.
- Execution should descend into `if-then` action units.
- Vision must remain compatible with psychological safety and should not silence risk or dissent.

## Not Used

- General blogs, Reddit, Wikipedia-like pages, and low-authority summaries were not used as the factual basis for this policy.
