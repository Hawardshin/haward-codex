## Applied Judgment

- Profitability judgment includes ROI, time horizon, discounting, break-even, unit economics, and scenario/sensitivity analysis.
- Numeric claims need source, date, currency, formula, and tax/fee inclusion notes.
- High-stakes financial decisions need professional review and a human checkpoint.

## Not Used

- Reddit, Wikipedia, and SEO calculator pages were not used as policy evidence.
- Commercial-blog benchmark numbers are treated as freshness-sensitive and require verification before real decisions.
